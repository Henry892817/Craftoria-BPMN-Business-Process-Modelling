# Craftoria – Business Process Modelling (BPMN)

## Project Overview

Craftoria is a conceptual online marketplace designed to connect independent creators of handmade products with customers.

This academic team project focuses on modelling business processes to demonstrate how different departments, users, and systems interact to support marketplace operations.

## Project Objectives

- Model and document essential business processes using BPMN.
- Visualize workflows, decisions, and interactions between departments.
- Analyze business operations and identify potential process improvements.
- Demonstrate business process management and organizational analysis skills.

## Business Processes Covered

1. Seller account creation
2. Product review and approval
3. Customer account creation
4. Customer order processing
5. Employee recruitment announcements
6. Employee hiring
7. Marketing campaign approval
8. Weekly campaign performance review
9. Customer product returns
10. Customer account inactivation
11. Seller account inactivation

## Skills Demonstrated

- Business Process Model and Notation (BPMN)
- Business Process Analysis
- Workflow Modelling
- Organizational Structure Analysis
- Process Documentation
- Cross-functional Collaboration
